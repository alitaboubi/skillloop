# «غدوة إن شاء الله» — Reel SkillLoop (Veo 3)

9:16 · photoréaliste · ~35 s au montage. Chaque clip Veo fait 8 s, on n'en garde que 1 à 4 s.
Mettre les clips choisis dans `marketing/reel-ep1/clips/` avec le nom de la scène (`v01.mp4`, `v02a.mp4`…).

## Règles (pour que ce soit « wow » et cohérent)
1. **Coller le bloc STYLE + le bloc du personnage tels quels** dans chaque prompt. C'est ce qui garde les mêmes visages et le même rendu cinéma d'un clip à l'autre.
2. **Montage de l'attente (V02a → V02e) : même cadrage fixe** dans les 5 prompts (« static locked-off camera, same framing »). Au montage, les coupes se font sur le même cadre et seuls la saison et le personnage changent : c'est l'effet le plus fort du reel.
3. **Pas de texte, pas de sous-titres, pas de logos.** Écrans de téléphone **jamais face caméra**, sticker QR **blanc et vide** : on incruste la vraie interface et le vrai QR SkillLoop au montage.
4. **2 à 4 variantes par scène**, garder la meilleure. Si le derja sort avec un accent égyptien ou du Golfe, garder l'image : on remplacera la voix.
5. Pas d'étoiles ni d'avis à l'écran (SkillLoop n'a pas encore d'avis clients).

## STYLE (à coller dans chaque prompt)
Vertical 9:16. Cinematic photorealism, shot on ARRI Alexa with 35mm anamorphic lenses, shallow depth of field, warm Mediterranean light, rich teal-and-amber color grade, subtle film grain, natural skin texture. Tunisian setting and people. No on-screen text, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.

## Personnages (à coller tels quels)
- **SI LAMJED**: a 58-year-old Tunisian man, round friendly face, thick black-and-grey mustache, receding grey hair, wearing a beige knitted cardigan over a striped shirt, grey trousers and leather house slippers. Very expressive comedic face.
- **HACHEM**: his 24-year-old nephew, Tunisian, short faded haircut, light stubble, white sneakers, black hoodie, confident smile.
- **KARIM**: a 30-year-old Tunisian plumber, athletic, neat short beard, clean navy blue work overalls without any logo, grey tool bag, warm professional smile.
- **SALMA**: a 32-year-old Tunisian woman, shoulder-length dark hair, cream trench coat, tote bag, curious and lively.

## V01 — Hook : la promesse (0–3 s)
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc]. A traditional Tunisian apartment hallway with blue and white zellige tiles and a wooden front door. A kitchen tap behind him sprays water. He holds an old phone to his ear, relieved and hopeful. From the phone, a confident male voice in Tunisian Arabic dialect says loudly: "غدوة الصباح نجيك إن شاء الله، كلمة رجال!". Si Lamjed nods and answers: "يعيشك، نستناك". Slow dolly-in on his hopeful face. Sound: dripping water, phone speaker voice, quiet apartment.

## V02a — Attente, jour 3
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc]. Static locked-off camera, wide frame: he sits on a wooden chair in the middle of the hallway, facing the closed front door, a glass of Tunisian mint tea in his hand, foot tapping impatiently. Morning light from the side. Sound: clock ticking, distant street noise.

## V02b — Attente, Ramadan
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc]. Static locked-off camera, exact same wide framing, same chair facing the same front door. It is Ramadan evening: a lit brass lantern (fanous) on the floor, he holds a bowl of chorba on his lap and eats slowly without taking his eyes off the door. Warm golden lamplight. Sound: distant call to prayer, spoon clinking.

## V02c — Attente, l'Aïd
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc]. Static locked-off camera, exact same wide framing, same chair facing the same front door. A white sheep with curly horns sits calmly next to the chair, and both man and sheep stare at the door at the same time, then slowly turn their heads toward each other. Deadpan comedy. Sound: a soft "baa", clock ticking.

## V02d — Attente, l'été
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc], but now wearing a straw hat, sunglasses and a colourful beach shirt. Static locked-off camera, exact same wide framing, same chair facing the same front door. A beach umbrella is planted next to the chair, an electric fan blows at him, he sweats and fans himself with a newspaper. Bright harsh summer light. Sound: cicadas, fan hum.

## V02e — Attente, l'hiver (le rappel)
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc], now with a long untidy grey beard, wrapped in a thick wool blanket. Static locked-off camera, exact same wide framing, same chair facing the same front door, cobwebs on the chair legs. Cold blue winter light, rain on the window. He picks up the phone and asks weakly in Tunisian Arabic dialect: "آلو… وقتاش؟". The same confident voice from the phone answers: "غدوة إن شاء الله!". He closes his eyes in despair. Sound: rain, wind, phone voice.

## V03 — Hachem arrive
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc], long beard and blanket. HACHEM: [bloc]. Hachem opens the front door, sees his uncle, freezes, then laughs and says in Tunisian Arabic dialect: "خالي، إنت مازلت تستنى؟!". He crouches next to him and shows him his smartphone (screen facing away from the camera). Handheld medium shot, slight push-in. Sound: door creak, laughter.

## V04 — Ding dong : il est venu (après l'écran « Karim est en route »)
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc], clean-shaven again, normal clothes. KARIM: [bloc]. The doorbell rings. Si Lamjed opens the front door; Karim stands there exactly on time with his tool bag and says politely in Tunisian Arabic dialect: "صباح الخير سي لمجد، جيت في الوقت!". Si Lamjed stares, speechless, then theatrically faints backwards onto a soft sofa behind him. Comedic slow-motion fall, camera low angle from inside the hallway. Sound: doorbell, a soft thud on cushions.

## V05 — « جيت؟ بالحق جيت؟ »
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc]. KARIM: [bloc]. A bright Tunisian kitchen with blue zellige tiles. Karim fixes the tap under the sink with a wrench, quickly and cleanly; water stops. Si Lamjed stands behind him, pokes Karim's shoulder with one finger as if checking he is real, and whispers in Tunisian Arabic dialect: "جيت؟ بالحق جيت؟". Karim smiles and turns on the tap: clear water flows perfectly. Close-ups, smooth gimbal movement. Sound: wrench clicks, running water.

## V06 — Le QR sur la vitrine
[STYLE] KARIM: [bloc]. Outside his small plumbing shop on a sunny Tunis street with whitewashed walls and blue shutters, morning. He presses a plain blank white square sticker onto the glass shop window and smooths it with his thumb, then steps back proudly with arms crossed. Macro shot of the thumb smoothing the sticker, then a wide shot. The sticker is completely blank. Sound: street ambience, birds.

## V07 — Salma scanne
[STYLE] SALMA: [bloc]. Same Tunis street and shop window with the blank white sticker. Salma walks past, notices the sticker, stops, scans it with her phone (screen facing away from the camera), her eyes widen, she smiles and calls to a friend off-screen in Tunisian Arabic dialect: "شوف خدمتو!". Shallow depth of field, gentle tracking shot. Sound: street ambience, phone camera click.

## V08 — Fin : la question (CTA commentaires)
[STYLE] SI LAMJED: [bloc]. He sits relaxed on his sofa with a fresh glass of mint tea, turns to the camera, raises an eyebrow and asks with a mischievous smile in Tunisian Arabic dialect: "وإنتوما؟ شكون قالكم غدوة وما جاش؟". Then he winks. Slow push-in to a close-up. Warm cozy light. Sound: calm room tone.
